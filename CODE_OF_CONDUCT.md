# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, and assume good intent. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer ([@firasmosbehi](https://github.com/firasmosbehi)) through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/firasmosbehi/mcp-exporter/security/advisories/new) titled "Code of conduct". Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or ban participants who violate this code.
